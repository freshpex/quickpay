# Motion and responsive acceptance checklist

Normal-motion design uses distinct treatments, rather than repeated opacity fades:

1. Coordinated hero headline and supporting-copy entrance with offset timing.
2. Hero product-preview float and changing rotation.
3. Breathing hand-drawn underline and large arrow rotation.
4. Continuous service/brand marquee, with global pause.
5. Scroll-sticky product cards with depth scaling and offset tops.
6. Rotating dashed product-orbit composition inside the cards.
7. Pinned product story with scroll-selected state, synchronized preview and copy.
8. Product-preview perspective transition between story states.
9. CTA supporting text changes with story state, stable destination/name/focus.
10. A real walking person in locally served footage; the media composition translates and rotates with scroll.
11. Gift-card fan, overlapping rotations, individual ambient movement and hover lift.
12. Referral progress connection moving through invite/payment/reward steps.
13. Referral node movement and button hover/press feedback.
14. Animated mobile marketing menu and customer drawer transition.

Marketing can be theatrical; payment controls remain stable, labelled and immediately usable. Request status uses live status semantics. All password fields share the accessible PasswordInput, including confirmation, transaction authorization and reset.

Browser screenshots are in `docs/screenshots`. The test harness checks approximately 360, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440px. A still screenshot proves composition, not continuous animation; the browser motion tests separately exercise state changes, reduced-motion behavior and media-failure usability.
